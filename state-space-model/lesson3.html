3.1 Two Levels of Sensitivity

Type-1 (object level): How well does recall discriminate remembered from forgotten items? Standard d’.

Type-2 (metacognitive level): How well does the participant’s confidence (JOL) discriminate their own correct from incorrect responses? Meta-d’.

From Maniscalco & Lau (2012): meta-d’ is “what d’ would need to be if the observer’s sensitivity were perfectly aligned with their confidence.”

\[M\text{-ratio} = \frac{\text{meta-}d'}{d'} \qquad \text{(metacognitive efficiency)}\]

M-ratio = 1.0 means perfect efficiency; < 1.0 means suboptimal access to the internal signal.
