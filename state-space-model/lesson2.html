2.1 The Two-Phase Fitting Strategy

From s02_fit_state_space_model.py:

  • Phase 1 — Grid search: Evaluate SSE at 50 α values across (0.001, 0.999)
  • Phase 2 — Refinement: minimize_scalar(method='bounded') starting from the grid region

The grid prevents boundary artifacts for subjects with flat or noisy SSE landscapes.
